HOW IT'S BUILT

FIVE LAYERS,
ONE PIPELINE

From camera frame to craving swap — here's every layer a single scan passes through.

THE STACK

SYSTEM LAYERS

L1

CAPTURE

The entry point for every session: the camera view for a scan, or a text/voice field for a craving. This layer's only job is getting raw input — a frame or a phrase — into the pipeline cleanly.

L2

PERCEPTION

Panel detection crops the ingredient block out of the frame, and OCR turns that crop into plain text. This is where pixels stop being pixels and start being words SafeBite can reason about.

L3

UNDERSTANDING

Extracted ingredients get checked against your allergen profile and flagged-additive list, while the nudge model reads your scan history in parallel to look for patterns worth surfacing.

L4

GENERATION

For cravings, this is where the craving VAE embeds your request and the swap ranker scores nearby products — turning "something sweet and fizzy" into an ordered list of real substitutes.

L5

INTERFACE

Everything the layers below produced — verdict, swap cards, proactive nudge — gets rendered back to you in plain language, no raw model output in sight.
