THE NOVELTY STATEMENT

WHAT'S NEW, PLAINLY

The scanning half of SafeBite — panel detection plus OCR — draws on computer-vision techniques that are already well established, and comparable to what apps like Yuka and Fig already ship. That part is not the novelty. What SafeBite adds is pairing that scan with a craving-aware swap engine that understands what you're actually in the mood for, and a proactive nudge layer that reads your scan history and speaks up before you've even opened the camera. The scan tells you about one product. The rest of SafeBite is built to notice the pattern across all of them.

STAYING HONEST

KNOWN LIMITATIONS

  • The scanning half — panel detection and OCR — is not novel; comparable techniques already ship in apps like Yuka and Fig.
  • The craving engine and swap-ranking model are unvalidated: no user study or held-out benchmark has confirmed the swaps are actually preferred.
  • The proactive nudge layer is trained on limited scan-history data, so its pattern-reading is still preliminary.
  • Every metric on the Results page is an in-progress placeholder, not a final, reproducible benchmark.
